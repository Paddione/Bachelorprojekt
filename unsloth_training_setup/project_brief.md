# Project Brief

- **Problem Definition**: Versatile model capable of general coding and complex reasoning for use in factory slots.
- **User Domain / Audience**: Internal AI factory slots (engineers/agents).
- **Chosen Base Model**: Gemma 26B QAT
- **Target Hardware**: NVIDIA 5070TI (16 GB VRAM)
- **Training Method**: GRPO (recommended for reasoning/coding) or SFT (depending on dataset availability).
